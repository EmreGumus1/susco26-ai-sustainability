Main Finding
A carbon- & confidence-aware Hybrid Router (batched 4-bit/MoE locally first; clean Nordic cloud only on hard uncertainty) cuts CO₂ by 95.6% (85.0 → 3.78 g/1k), whereas unbatched local CPU/GPU inference in coal grids emits 5×–8× more than Sweden cloud.
